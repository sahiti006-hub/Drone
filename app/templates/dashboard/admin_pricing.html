📐 Mathematical Pricing Model

Deterministic Pricing Pipeline:

Quotes are calculated transparently with Decimal precision to prevent floating-point discrepancies:

Subtotal = BaseFare + (Distance_km × PricePerKm) + (Payload_kg × PricePerKg) + PriorityFee + ServiceFee Clamped = Clamp(Subtotal, MinFare, MaxFare) Tax = Clamped × (TaxRatePercent / 100) TotalPrice = Clamped + Tax
